The Man Who Sold His Skin, screened in London

An evening with director Kaouther Ben Hania and producer Nadim Cheikhrouha at Ciné Lumière.

Screening · May 2025

On 30 May 2025 we invited audiences to a special screening of The Man Who Sold His Skin at the Institut Français's Ciné Lumière in London, followed by a live discussion with director Kaouther Ben Hania and producer Nadim Cheikhrouha.

The Oscar-nominated film follows Sam Ali, a young Syrian who fled the war to Lebanon and, to reach Europe and the woman he loves, lets a contemporary artist tattoo his back — becoming a work of art and confronting the price of freedom in a world of borders and markets.

Guillaume Rambourg is credited as an associate producer of the film, the start of our long collaboration with Kaouther Ben Hania.

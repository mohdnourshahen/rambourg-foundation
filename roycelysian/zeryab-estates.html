The project

A Sanctuary of Elegance and Tranquility

A private luxury retreat in Marrakech, where Andalusian, Berber and Arab culture meet in something singular. Long arcades, carved cedar, still water and rooms where art and architecture keep the same quiet discipline. We photographed the estate the way it was built, with patience and symmetry, and the brand identity was created in collaboration with Lōn Visuals.

Heritage, reimagined

Alongside the photography, the identity of Zeryab Estates was developed in collaboration with Lōn Visuals. The brief called for an identity that could carry centuries of culture without collapsing under its weight, and speak to an international audience while staying rooted in its place.

The mark brings together two of Morocco’s most enduring forms, the Moorish arch and the interlocking geometry of zellige, in one architectural emblem. A wide-tracked wordmark gives the name the presence of an inscription.

Palette

Six tones taken from Marrakech: the navy of tile shadow, the umber of cedarwood, the gold of brass lanterns, the tan of sun-dried clay, handmade terracotta and poured iron.

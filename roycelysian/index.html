The Studio

Roycelysian is a creative studio for photography, branding and editorial design, working with exceptional properties, luxury destinations and premium brands.

We begin by understanding a place: its character, its history, its architecture and the people it is for. Then we build the visual language that lets it be seen, and remembered, for what it is.

Built on years of work across Morocco, Spain, Saudi Arabia and Jordan. A select number of projects at a time, one team, one point of view.

Three disciplines.
One creative vision.

Photography and design stand as two independent practices. Creative direction and storytelling bring them together, so a place or a brand can be shaped, captured and told in a single voice. Engage us for one, or for all of it.

01

Photography & Film

Editorial-standard imagery, built on light, composition, colour and the feeling of a place.Architectural · Interiors & Exteriors · Hospitality · Commercial · Lifestyle · Aerial · Film · Retouching & Colour Grading

02

Branding & Design

Identities that express the character of a brand or a place, carried through every point of contact, including the space itself.Brand Strategy · Naming · Visual Identity · Art Direction · Signage & Wayfinding · Brand Applications · Print & Digital Design

03

Editorial Publishing

Bespoke books and publications that hold the identity, character and detail of their subject.Coffee Table Books · Architectural Monographs · Property Books · Brand Books · Collector's Editions

Websites, creative direction & copywriting, and visual consulting complete the picture within a project. When an identity is carried into a space, we design the applications and supervise them; fabrication and installation are delivered with specialist partners.

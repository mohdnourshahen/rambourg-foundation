The project

Capturing the Essence of Exclusivity

Royal L'Azure belongs to L'Azure Hospitality, a premium hotel group on the Saudi Red Sea coast. Arches, reflections and a measured, almost ceremonial symmetry: we photographed the resort's spaces to hold that calm, and the group's identity was created in collaboration with Lōn Visuals.

Where the sea meets luxury

For L'Azure Hospitality, the group behind Royal L'Azure and Sharma Resort, the identity was developed in collaboration with Lōn Visuals: one system to evoke the calm of water, the warmth of sand and the depth of the sea.

It is built around a fluid wave symbol that holds movement and calm at once, carried from embossed stationery and room keys to the welcome kit and the group’s website.

Palette

Dark blue against tan gold, deep water against warm sand, held together by a deeper navy, black and white.

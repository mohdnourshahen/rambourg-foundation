Who we are

Roycelysian is a creative studio specialising in photography, branding and editorial design for exceptional properties, luxury destinations and premium brands.

Built on years of creative experience and a portfolio of work across Morocco, Spain, Saudi Arabia and Jordan, we bring together visual expertise, considered design and storytelling to create distinctive visual experiences.

Our work begins with understanding the character of each place or brand. Through creative direction, photography, refined image editing and identity development, we build a cohesive visual language that reflects its individuality, and carry it from brand identities and websites to large-format books.

What we do

Photography. Branding. Editorial design.

Three disciplines, one creative vision. We create identities, capture spaces and tell stories through imagery, design and thoughtfully produced publications.

Why Roycelysian

One studio. One point of view.

There are excellent architectural photographers, excellent branding agencies and excellent book designers. Rarely under one creative vision.

Instead of dividing a project between a photographer, an identity designer, a writer and a book designer, our clients work with one team and one direction, and can still engage any discipline on its own. We take on a select number of projects at a time, and stay close to each one.

We compete on vision and craft, not on volume or speed.
